# Production state sampler audit

**Scope and boundary.** This checked, reporting-only inventory records the bounded production audit after the `7luh` handoff barrier and `cad4` immutable session-exit work. This document describes landed behavior only; it does not redesign pool APIs, eviction/kill behavior, retention, routing, retry, or attempt accounting.

## No-transit-state-conviction contract

A sampler may convict or destructively act on a transit-capable state only with authoritative terminal state, an advancing state-entry timestamp older than a justified bound, or positive evidence that the exact owner is absent. Unknown or failed evidence acquisition preserves state; creation-only timestamps are invalid.

**Reusable safe-mutation/test pattern.** Before mutation, identify the exact owner and read the authoritative terminal state, or read an advancing timestamp for entry into the currently sampled state and compare it with the documented bound, or obtain positive exact-owner absence evidence. Treat every unavailable, ambiguous, stale, error, `Unknown`, or `NotApplicable` result as preserve-state unless it is itself authoritative terminal evidence. Tests must drive each evidence-acquisition failure and ambiguous branch, assert no destructive action occurs, and separately prove that the authorized terminal, aged advancing-transition, and exact-owner-absence branches may mutate the addressed owner only. A creation timestamp may supply context or a lower bound, but never the state-entry clock that authorizes conviction.

## Reproducible search vocabulary

The inventory was produced on the checked tree with this bounded vocabulary. Each production hit was traced to a scheduled, startup, event, RPC, or operator-facing caller and is either represented below or accounted for in the exclusions ledger.

```sh
rg -n -i '\b(stale|stranded|orphan|dead|reap|sweep|retention|timeout|idle|absence|kill|release|interrupt)\b' \
  server/crates/**/src --glob '*.rs'
```

Primary seams are coordinator recovery/health, database reapers, `djinn-slot` pool handle/actor, and the shared `djinn_core::job_retention` policy. “Exact owner absence” means the addressed run/session/intent is proved absent or terminal; a missing process-local map entry alone is not that proof. “Authoritative state-entry time” is the timestamp of the relevant current state, not an unrelated creation time.

## Follow-ups for unsafe findings

- **FOLLOW-UP-STUCK-ZOMBIE-NOT-APPLICABLE** — require configured exact Job/session absence proof before destructive mutation for a nonterminal task run; preserve unsupported no-task-run shape and existing policies.
- **FOLLOW-UP-STALE-TASK-RUN-PERIODIC** — replace creation-time-only periodic conviction with advancing progress/state time or successful exact owner absence proof.
- **FOLLOW-UP-STALE-TASK-RUN-STARTUP** — require exact current owner/Job proof or durable restart handoff rather than restart plus age.
- **FOLLOW-UP-PENDING-ATTEMPT-PERIODIC** — fail closed on NULL, malformed, missing, or lookup-error owner evidence while retaining exact-group atomicity for proved cases.
- **FOLLOW-UP-PENDING-ATTEMPT-STARTUP** — preserve different-incarnation proof but make ambiguous same/unknown ownership non-destructive.
- **FOLLOW-UP-REFINEMENT-READ-COMMITTED-SNAPSHOT** — obtain exact run liveness under one coherent snapshot or equivalent positive owner proof, retaining locks/fence/admission semantics.
- **FOLLOW-UP-SLOT-POOL-MAP-ONLY-CONVICTION** — enforce that a negative `has_session` remains an observation and cannot alone terminalize/release work; keep existing cluster-witness and fail-on-ask-error composition.
- **FOLLOW-UP-BOARD-RECONCILE-POOL-AMNESIA** — make `board_reconcile_impl` require successful exact session/Job-owner absence evidence before its negative-pool-map branch persists an active session as interrupted; retain its existing wedged/runtime-cap handling, RPC error propagation, and dispatch policy.
- **FOLLOW-UP-SLOT-POOL-EVICTION-BEST-EFFORT** — scope a separate failure-atomicity review of evict/reclaim ordering and whether failed durable/Job operations may safely permit redispatch; do not change kill, routing, retry, or retention behavior in this audit.

## Exclusions ledger

- **Tests, fixtures, and test-only support:** `#[cfg(test)]`, `*_tests.rs`, fixture/backdate helpers, and test-only pool messages are regression evidence, never production samplers.
- **Pool/slot lifecycle mechanics not making a stale verdict:** dispatch, pause, direct operator `kill_session`/`terminate_session`, reconfigure/drain, `Free`/`Killed` capacity accounting, and status/snapshot APIs are included only where the `has_session`, eviction, or reconciliation paths above consume or expose a sampler verdict; otherwise they are execution mechanics.
- **Control-plane rendering/reporting:** dashboard, board/session listings, evidence display, debug snapshots, and telemetry may describe stale/idle/dead state but are excluded when they only render/report. `board_reconcile_impl` is not an ordinary view path: its `has_session` negative branch terminalizes ownership and is included above; the listed control-plane reconciliation RPC is likewise included.
- **Kubernetes/runtime infrastructure:** workload inventory, eviction diagnostics, launcher/runtime creation, warm jobs, graph warming, image-controller retention, cache/target-run pruning, cgroup child reaping, log rotation, and filesystem GC manage infrastructure/cache resources rather than convict a task/session/attempt transit workflow state.
- **Provider, prompt, LLM, streaming, compaction, workspace/git, graph/index, memory, QA, MCP, and parser hits:** vocabulary occurrences describe retry/timeouts/retention/local cleanup or user-facing diagnostics, but have no scheduled/startup/event/RPC workflow-owner verdict followed by task/session/attempt terminalization, release, or exact Job deletion; excluded.
- **Database/history retention:** output stash, terminal transcript, audit/evidence history, CI/reporting retention, and immutable terminal-history cleanup use already-terminal history or storage policy, not a mutable workflow-state conviction; excluded.
- **Pure helpers/classifiers:** `djinn-core` liveness/refinement/retention helpers without a mutating caller are not standalone samplers. The shared Job-retention helper is included above because all three production deletion caller families are traced; other pure DTO/parser helpers are excluded.
