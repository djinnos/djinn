# Production state sampler audit

**Scope and boundary.** This is the initial, reporting-only coordinator/database inventory after the `7luh` handoff barrier and `cad4` truthful, immutable session-exit work. A sampler may not convict a mutable transit state merely because it is old: terminal evidence, an advancing state-entry time, or positive evidence that the exact owner is absent is required. This document records the code as landed; it does not alter retry, kill, routing, attempt accounting, or sampler behavior.

## Reproducible search vocabulary

The inventory was produced on the checked tree with the bounded vocabulary below, then each production hit was traced to its scheduled, startup, event, or RPC caller before inclusion or exclusion:

```sh
rg -n -i '\b(stale|stranded|orphan|dead|reap|sweep|retention|timeout|idle|absence|kill|release|interrupt)\b' \
  server/crates/**/src --glob '*.rs'
```

Primary reviewed seams are `server/crates/djinn-coordinator/src/actor.rs`, `dispatch/session_recovery.rs`, `dispatch/liveness.rs`, and `health.rs`, plus `server/crates/djinn-db/src/repositories/task_run.rs`, `task_attempt.rs`, and `refinement_run.rs`. “Exact owner absence” means an addressed run/session/intent is proved absent or terminal; a missing process-local map entry alone is not that proof. “Authoritative state-entry time” is the timestamp of the relevant current state, not an unrelated creation time.

## Follow-ups for unsafe findings

- **FOLLOW-UP-STUCK-ZOMBIE-NOT-APPLICABLE** — affected entry point: scheduled `CoordinatorActor::enforce_session_stall_timeout` and its ready-/execution-state orphan branches. Remediation boundary: do not let `ClusterWitness::NotApplicable` authorize destructive session/attempt/task mutation when a nonterminal durable task run could be the exact owner; require a configured exact Job/session absence proof or otherwise fail closed. Preserve the explicitly unsupported no-task-run shape and do not change retry, kill, routing, or attempt-accounting policy in this reporting-only audit.
- **FOLLOW-UP-STALE-TASK-RUN-PERIODIC** — affected entry point: `health::reap_stale_task_runs` / `TaskRunRepository::reap_stale_running`. Remediation boundary: replace the periodic creation-time-only conviction with an advancing run-state/progress timestamp or successful exact Job/session absence proof; leave retry, attempt outcomes, and routing unchanged.
- **FOLLOW-UP-STALE-TASK-RUN-STARTUP** — affected entry point: `health::reap_stale_task_runs_for_startup` / `TaskRunRepository::reap_stale_running`. Remediation boundary: make startup use an exact current-owner/Job proof or durable restart handoff evidence before terminal mutation; do not treat coordinator restart plus row age as death.
- **FOLLOW-UP-PENDING-ATTEMPT-PERIODIC** — affected entry point: `health::reap_orphaned_pending_attempts_core` periodic branch. Remediation boundary: fail closed on NULL, malformed, missing, or lease-lookup-error owner evidence instead of recording a crashed attempt; retain exact-group atomicity and existing outcomes for proven cases.
- **FOLLOW-UP-PENDING-ATTEMPT-STARTUP** — affected entry point: `health::reap_orphaned_pending_attempts_for_startup` and shared core. Remediation boundary: preserve the single-leader different-incarnation proof but make ambiguous same/unknown owner evidence non-destructive; do not alter respawn, retry, or strike policy in this audit.

## Exclusions ledger

- **Tests and fixtures:** `#[cfg(test)]` modules and fixture/backdate helpers (for example `TaskRunRepository::backdate_started_at`) were searched only as regression evidence, never counted as production samplers.
- **UI-only rendering:** task/chat list projections and dashboard rendering can display stale/idle state but do not terminalize, release, kill, or reap workflow ownership; excluded.
- **Immutable-history retention:** output-stash and terminal transcript retention in `health.rs` uses terminal `ended_at` history and arming/report-only policy; it does not convict a live transit workflow state, so excluded from this workflow sampler inventory.
- **Age/size cache cleanup:** cargo target run directories, sccache, warm-base/cache health, and filesystem GC are cache/resource cleanup rather than task/session/attempt ownership verdicts; excluded.
- **Unreachable pure classifiers:** `dispatch/liveness.rs` is side-effect-free and is included only through its reachable session-exit consumer. Pure DTO/classifier branches without a scheduled, startup, event, or RPC mutation consumer are excluded as non-samplers.
